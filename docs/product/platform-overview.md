# RecAI Platform Overview

## Product Thesis

AI has made it easier for candidates to generate polished resumes, project descriptions, and application materials. That raises the difficulty for genuine candidates trying to stand out and for recruiters trying to trust what they are reading.

RecAI creates a more credible hiring signal by shifting part of the candidate story away from self-reporting and toward verified external recommendations from people who actually worked with the candidate.

## Core User Types

### Candidates

- Create a profile
- Add relevant projects and roles
- Join job postings they are interested in
- Request recommendations from managers, coworkers, or peers
- Control which recommendations appear on their profile, but not the content of those recommendations

### Recommenders

- Receive a recommendation request
- Verify identity through work email and an access code
- Fill out a structured recommendation with AI assistance
- Do not need a full product account

### Recruiters

- Create job postings
- Search only within the candidate pool tied to their posting
- Evaluate candidates using validated technical and behavioral signals
- Use structured filters plus natural-language retrieval over recommendation-backed candidate data

## Integrity Model

- Candidates cannot edit what recommenders say about them.
- Recommenders must verify through work email before submitting.
- Recruiters search against evidence rooted in external recommendations and concrete work, not just self-described claims.

## Experience Principles

- Keep the product user-first and credibility-first.
- Treat recommendations as supporting evidence, not vanity testimonials.
- Do not replace resumes; add a stronger layer of validation on top of them.
- Make recruiter search feel powerful without forcing rigid categorization on the recruiter.
